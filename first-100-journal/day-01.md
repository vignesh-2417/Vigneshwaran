# Day 1 — I made my first $100 online without showing my face

**Length:** 28–35 seconds  
**Post:** TikTok first, then same file to Reels + Shorts

## Hook (first frame)

```
I made $100 online.
No face. No followers.
```

## Voiceover (copy)

I made my first 100 dollars online without showing my face.
Not crypto. Not a course. Not “passive income.”
Three boring steps.
One: I picked one tiny problem people already pay to solve. Resume help. Caption writing. Simple Canva posts.
Two: I posted the same offer every day for 14 days. Same words. Same price. 20 dollars a job.
Three: I asked every buyer, “Who else needs this?” That’s how 5 jobs became 100 dollars.
The secret is not AI. It’s a small offer, a real person, and daily posts until someone says yes.
