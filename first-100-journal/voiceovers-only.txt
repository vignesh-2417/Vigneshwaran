FIRST $100 JOURNAL — VOICEOVERS ONLY
Paste one DAY block at a time into ElevenLabs or your phone recorder.
Speak calm and slightly fast. Cut pauses over 0.4s in CapCut.


===== DAY 1 =====
I made my first 100 dollars online without showing my face.
Not crypto. Not a course. Not “passive income.”
Three boring steps.
One: I picked one tiny problem people already pay to solve. Resume help. Caption writing. Simple Canva posts.
Two: I posted the same offer every day for 14 days. Same words. Same price. 20 dollars a job.
Three: I asked every buyer, “Who else needs this?” That’s how 5 jobs became 100 dollars.
The secret is not AI. It’s a small offer, a real person, and daily posts until someone says yes.


===== DAY 2 =====
This is the exact message I sent.
Not “hey.” Not a paragraph.
“I help people fix one thing: a resume, a caption, or 3 Instagram posts. 20 dollars. 24 hours. If you want it, say YES.”
I sent it to 20 people who already asked for help in comments.
3 said yes. That is 60 dollars. Two more later. First 100.
If they ignore you, send it again in 3 days. One line. Same price.


===== DAY 3 =====
Five 20-dollar offers beginners can sell this week. No face.
One: rewrite a resume headline.
Two: 10 TikTok captions for their business.
Three: clean their Instagram bio.
Four: turn one messy idea into a 30-second script.
Five: 5 Canva posts, same style.
Pick one. Not five. Post it every day. 20 dollars times 5 jobs is your first 100.


===== DAY 4 =====
Nobody pays you on day 1.
Day 1 to 5: silence. That is normal.
Day 6: most people delete the app and buy another course.
Day 8 to 14: that is when the first YES usually shows up.
I got ignored for 11 days. Job 1 came on day 12.
The algorithm is not the enemy. Your calendar is. Post the same offer for 14 days or you never gave it a chance.


===== DAY 5 =====
14 days. Same offer. No face.
Views: some videos 200, one hit 4,000.
DMs: 20 sent.
Replies: 7.
Paying customers: 5.
Money: 100 dollars.
That is not viral. That is a small shop.
Viral is luck. A 20-dollar offer is a system. Follow if you want the system, not the lottery.


===== DAY 6 =====
A 97-dollar course feels like progress. It is not income.
Your first 100 should be cash from a stranger, not notes from a guru.
One paid job teaches you more than 10 hours of YouTube.
Use free tools. Phone. CapCut. Canva free.
Then sell something this week. Even ugly. Even 20 dollars.
Knowledge in your head is worth zero until someone pays.


===== DAY 7 =====
Your week-one plan. Screenshot this.
Pick one 20-dollar offer from video 3.
Write the one-line DM from video 2.
Post once a day. Same offer. Same price.
Send 10 messages a day to people who already want help.
Do not change the offer. Do not buy a new tool.
Day 15: count the money. If it is not 100 yet, run the same 14 days again.
That is the whole journal. Follow so you don’t do it alone.
